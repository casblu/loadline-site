How live is this?

What updates itself, and what does not

Automatic (every 6 hours)

A scheduled GitHub Action re-downloads Epoch AI's open CSVs, rebuilds data/ai-datacenters.json, geocodes any new address, and stamps each field with a fetched time. ERCOT's public grid dashboard is refreshed hourly (system-wide, not per data center).

Only as live as Epoch

Epoch updates its dataset itself, roughly weekly, from satellite imagery and filings. Our status labels are derived from the latest dated row of Epoch's timeline, so a change in the real world shows up only after Epoch records it. "As of" is the date of that observation, not today.

Manual

Company statements, paused/cancelled status and corrections are added by hand with a source. Nothing about a company is inferred. See data/ai-dc-manual.json. See a change? Report an update (public GitHub issue, source required) ↗

Limits

  • Epoch covers the largest sites (roughly 44% of global AI compute by its own estimate), not every data center.
  • Capacity is IT power in MW, not total facility power. Epoch says 80% of estimates fall within about 1.4x, compute within 1.5x.
  • Utility/grid-queue (ERCOT, PJM) large-load data, state filings and SEC filings are not machine-readable here yet, so power source is "not disclosed" unless Epoch lists an energy company.
  • Pins are geocoded from address text and can be wrong; the precision label says how good the match is.
  • Nobody has been contacted for this tracker.

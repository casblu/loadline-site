How it works, and limits

  • Values are scaled between the series' own minimum and maximum, then mapped to the chosen scale, volume, tempo or brightness. Different series are not comparable by ear: the lowest note is always that series' lowest value.
  • Series longer than 240 points are averaged into 240 buckets so playback stays short.
  • ERCOT today and ERCOT hourly come from ERCOT's public dashboards, fetched hourly by this repo's Action. The hourly history only started when this page was built (seeded from earlier hourly commits) and grows over time. System-wide figures only: data-center load is not metered separately.
  • AI data center series are ranked lists of sites, not time series. Almost all figures are Epoch AI estimates (CC BY 4.0), not company-reported numbers. Sites without a figure are skipped.
  • Sound is made with the Web Audio API in your browser. Browsers need a click or key press before audio can start. Phones may stay silent in silent mode.
  • The module is plain JavaScript: sonify.js. It takes any array of {t, v, label} points.
